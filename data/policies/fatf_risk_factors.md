# FATF Risk-Factor Summary (Illustrative)

Document ID: POL-FATF-003 | Version 1.0

## Customer Risk Factors
- PEP status, adverse media, complex ownership structures, cash-intensive business.

## Product / Channel Risk
- Anonymous channels, wire transfers to high-risk jurisdictions, rapid movement of funds.

## Geographic Risk
- Countries with weak AML controls or subject to sanctions carry elevated risk weighting.

## Risk Scoring Guidance
Combine customer, product, and geographic factors. A sanctions or PEP indicator alone is
sufficient to push a transaction into the CRITICAL band requiring human review.
